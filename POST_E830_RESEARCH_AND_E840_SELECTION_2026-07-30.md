# Post-E830 research and E840 selection

E750, E770, and E830 all show the same pattern: a global or session-level
probability prior may preserve or slightly improve ranking, but it damages
proper probabilities in `V_seen` and `V_style`. E810 and E820 show that
productive-talk cues and better math retrieval also do not supply the missing
outcome signal. The next branch must therefore carry independent real outcome
evidence before competition validation.

APTA is not the only possible source. The already authorised StudyChat
snapshot contains three future proctored exams, prior assignment grades, and
chronological dialogues for 175 matched learners across two semesters.
Earlier project work closed direct dialogue-act transfer because coefficients
were unstable and categories overlap tutor-move/uptake branches. It did not
test whether a fixed, label-free behaviour representation adds longitudinal
exam prediction beyond prior grades and usage controls.

E840 is that missing test. It is deliberately strict about chronology,
unseen-learner grouping, both semesters, all exams, proper residual baselines,
bootstrap evidence, and target-free source-distribution overlap. Failure
closes the exact StudyChat longitudinal path without spending a competition
outcome. Role-error correction remains a later target-free reserve, but its
small expected prevalence makes it lower priority than a real longitudinal
outcome source.
