# Post-E820 research and E830 selection

E820 established that a large target-free retrieval gain does not imply a
better correctness representation: adapted MathDial retrieval improved
confusion MRR by `0.115735`, but the frozen competition blend regressed mean
log loss by `0.001789`. The next branch therefore must encode a hypothesised
outcome cause, not merely improve semantic matching.

The project search found only the rejected BGE k-nearest-objective difficulty
prior (`0.600951` standalone hard loss and zero useful blend weight), sparse
objective token crosses, and linear objective alignment. No official
curriculum stage, grade progression, or taxonomy representation exists.

The Department for Education programme of study supplies an authorised,
openly licensed external sequence for the exact subject and age range. It
explicitly describes increasingly complex practice and gives year-by-year
content through Year 6 followed by KS3/KS4. That makes curriculum progression
the strongest remaining immediate test of the known objective-difficulty gap.

E830 is intentionally only a source/representation audit first. If its frozen
external sequencing, coverage, anchor, nonredundancy, or synthetic
proper-score clause fails, it will consume no competition validation and will
be closed literally. APTA remains a later independent source if access is
granted; E830 does not wait for or depend on APTA.
