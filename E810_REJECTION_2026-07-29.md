## Interpretation

The fitted coefficient directions were stable and mostly reproduced the
organizer's qualitative result: more student words and digit characters were
positive, while number-bearing turns per word were negative. That validates the
feature implementation but does not create useful residual probability signal
over v0.5. The severe `V_seen` and `V_style` proper-score regressions show that a
weak session-style head pulls the stronger raw v0.5 probabilities toward a
worse estimate. Target-free nonredundancy is therefore not enough: the feature
must add conditional outcome information after the champion, and E810 does not.

Adding the locally observed mean loss regression to public v0.5 gives a rough,
non-guaranteed public projection near `0.6074`. Extrapolating only from the
visible top-10 leaderboard density suggests roughly `#15–#30`, worse than the
current approximately `#11`; this is an uncertainty bracket, not a leaderboard
observation. E810 is not submission-worthy.
