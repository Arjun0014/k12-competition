# E890 MathRoBERTa semantic pre-screen rejection

## Decision

Reject the exact E890 semantic-representation direction before competition
outcomes. Do not build a competition cache, fit a competition probe, open
`V_seen`, `V_objective`, `V_style`, `V_joint`, or `V_final`, or package this
representation.

downloaded. The released optimizer and training-state pickle files were not

encoder; its unused language-model head was deliberately ignored.

## Target-free pre-screen

The deterministic pre-screen used 64 evenly spaced rows from the official
MathDial test table. It consumed only question identifiers for retrieval
matching, teacher-described confusion text, and dialogue text. It used no
MathDial correctness label and no competition text or outcome.

| Representation | Confusion MRR | Recall@5 | Off-diagonal cosine mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| BGE-base comparator | `0.3294654` | `0.4531250` | not applicable |
| MathRoBERTa CLS | `0.0582674` | `0.0937500` | `0.9893799` |
| MathRoBERTa mean | `0.1242145` | `0.1718750` | `0.9985400` |
| 75% BGE + 25% MathRoBERTa CLS | `0.3309711` | `0.4531250` | not applicable |
| 75% BGE + 25% MathRoBERTa mean | `0.3297789` | `0.4531250` | not applicable |

The raw embeddings are severely anisotropic and weak for the objective-to-
dialogue retrieval mechanism. Fixed 25% fusion adds effectively no target-free
value. This fails the required plausibility test for a robust `0.0016`
competition log-loss gain.

This rejection covers MathRoBERTa CLS/mean semantic replacement and their fixed
25% BGE fusions. It does not authorize centering, whitening, layer/pooling
sweeps, outcome fine-tuning, ALTER-Math label reuse, or another representation
rescue.
