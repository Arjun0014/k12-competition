# Post-E840 research and E850 selection

E840 showed that StudyChat behaviour transfers at the feature-distribution
level but not as stable longitudinal outcome evidence once prior grades,
usage, chronology, and unseen learners are controlled. That branch is closed,
and APTA remains pending rather than being treated as the only hope.

The strongest remaining local mechanism is the one explicitly left open by
the organizer clarification and the project log: correction of rare
student/tutor attribution errors. Earlier hard role prefixing lost value
because it fragmented shared lexical evidence and amplified mislabels. E850
does not repeat that architecture. It first asks whether roles can be recovered
from the utterance text, neighboring roles, length, and position without using
the current label as an input; then it measures how many high-confidence
contradictions exist across every target-free style cell.

This path is lower-ceiling than a rich approved APTA trajectory, so its gate
requires a correction opportunity large enough to plausibly affect at least
`0.0016` loss, but bounded enough to remain consistent with the organizer's
statement that errors are not widespread. Failure closes E850 without any
competition outcome.
